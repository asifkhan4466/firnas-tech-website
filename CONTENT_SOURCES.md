## Data decisions

- Removed the original sample telephone number, hello@ email, 50+ projects, 30+ clients, 5+ years and 100% commitment statistic.
- Use the explicit homepage team count of 60+. Other pages display different talent-pool counts; these are not combined.
- Eight service categories and six delivery stages are counts of the published lists, not customer or project counts.
- Use the Sharjah address from the homepage and the first contact-page listing for the UAE office. The contact page also contains a different Dubai listing; it is not presented as an additional office.
- Only LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram profiles linked by the official website are included. Unverified accounts, including YouTube, are omitted.
- The provided final logo is retained. Official website media replaces mockup crops and third-party stock imagery.
- Contact and newsletter forms remain frontend demos, as requested; they do not send or store submissions. Direct mail and telephone links use verified contact details.
